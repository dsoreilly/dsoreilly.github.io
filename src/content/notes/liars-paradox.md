The _liar's paradox_ is a logical and philosophical puzzle. In its most basic form, a contradicting statement:

> This statement is false.

There are two possible conclusions, both of which are contradictions:

1. The statement is **false**, therefore _"This statement is false."_ is **true**.
2. The statement is **true**, therefore _"This statement is false."_ is **false**.
